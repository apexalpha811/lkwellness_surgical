What the margins pay for

Cost of goods runs $199,412 a month against $731,666 of revenue, a blended 72.7% margin. What they cover:

The project is funded entirely with equity. There is no lender, no debt service and therefore no coverage ratio: every dollar of operating income accrues to the equity, and nothing is senior to it.

Unlevered yield on equity is stabilized annual operating income of $2,274,497 divided by the $5,000,000 raised. It is stated before tax, before any distribution policy, before maintenance capital and before any assumption about a sale, so it is not an investor return. No internal rate of return or exit multiple appears anywhere in this document, because nothing in this model supports a terminal value that could be sourced.

Cumulative cash across the modelled 36 months is $3,154,780. That figure includes the $540,000 of founding-member prepayments collected during construction, which is why the first year ends positive on a negative operating year. The equity is repaid out of that cash in month 46, which is past the end of the 36-month model and is therefore extrapolated at the stabilized run rate.

The working capital reserve of $1,054,800 is computed, not chosen: it is sized against a modelled cash trough of $607,422 at month 11.
